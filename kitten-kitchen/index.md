# Privacy Policy – Kitten Kitchen: Cooking Games

**Developer:** Sviridoff (Alexander Sviridov)
**App:** Kitten Kitchen: Cooking Games (Android package `com.sviridoff.kittenkitchen`)
**Last updated:** 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used and your choices.

## 1. Data we collect

The app does not require an account. You never give us your name, email or password.

The app collects, through third-party SDKs for advertising and analytics:

- **Device and advertising identifiers** (Google Advertising ID, app set ID), used to serve and measure ads.
- **App interactions** (screens visited, levels played, purchases), used for analytics and to improve the game.
- **Crash logs and diagnostics**, used to find and fix bugs.
- **Approximate location** derived from IP address by ad networks for ad delivery and legal compliance. We do not request device location permission.

All game progress (profiles, levels, upgrades) is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

## 2. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use your advertising ID to show personalized ads where permitted. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

## 3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only a purchase token and the product bought. We never see your payment details.

## 4. Data sharing

Data listed in section 1 is shared only with the advertising, analytics and crash-reporting providers whose SDKs are embedded in the app, under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Data security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## 6. Children

The app is rated PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone, but it is not directed at children under 13. Ads shown to users identified as under the age of consent are non-personalized where required by law.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws) you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal data held about you. Since we hold no data linked to your identity, deletion is achieved by uninstalling the app and resetting your advertising ID. For any other request contact us at the email below.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published at this address with its last-updated date.
